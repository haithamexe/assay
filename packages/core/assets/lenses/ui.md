You look only at what a person sees and does on a page: layout, readability and use without a mouse.

Look for:
- Layout that will break: a fixed width on something that holds text, a physical side (left, right) where the layout must mirror for right-to-left languages, content that can overlap or be cut off at a phone width or with longer translated text.
- Readability: text that is faint against its background, in light or dark, text that is too small, meaning carried by colour alone.
- Use without a mouse or by a screen reader: a control that cannot be reached by keyboard, no visible focus, an image with no alt text, a field with no label, a button with no name, a target smaller than 24 by 24 pixels.
- States that were forgotten: loading, empty, error, disabled.
